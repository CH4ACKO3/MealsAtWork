# Meals at Work 0.5.4

- Eat carried meals while crafting or researching at 80% work speed.
- Automatic crafting lunch breaks preserve unfinished work and resume progress.
- Hungry crafters can request delivery directly to their inventories; orders expire after two game hours by default.
- Empty worktables offer dining spots with configurable distance weighting.
- Support player mechanoids with an existing food need and standard work/eating interfaces.
- Optional Common Sense, Pick Up And Haul and Schedule Everything integrations.

HardworkingKz animal workers, Hospitality guests and fully custom job drivers are not yet supported. This initial automated Workshop release is private for testing.
