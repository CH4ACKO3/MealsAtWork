# Meals at Work 0.6.0

- Couriers collect up to six meals per trip and deliver them to multiple workers, choosing food for each recipient's diet.
- Colonists, hauling mechs and trained hauling animals can deliver meals.
- Hungry crafters and researchers place orders when a courier is available. Workers resume looking for food if all couriers become unavailable.
- Each order keeps its own deadline. A cancelled stop is skipped while the rest of the route continues; interrupted couriers return food and release their orders.
- Delivery routes and individual meal assignments survive saving and loading.
- Refreshed English and Chinese descriptions introduce the mod's features directly.
